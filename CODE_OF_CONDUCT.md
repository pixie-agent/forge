# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Pixie Agent welcomes respectful participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be constructive, focus criticism on the work, respect privacy, and assume good faith while still addressing harmful behavior directly.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, publishing another person's private information, sustained disruption, and retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, community spaces, and private communications made in the context of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **support@1forgen.site**. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, or ban an account when needed to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Anyone involved in a report must disclose conflicts of interest and recuse themselves where appropriate.
